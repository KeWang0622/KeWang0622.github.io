# 3D-world entrance illustration

`world-entrance-dusk.webp` and `world-entrance-dusk-640.webp` are decorative
AI-generated concept artwork used on the homepage entrance, not gameplay
screenshots. On 29 October 2026 the owner requested a clearer MRI identity.
OpenAI's built-in image-generation tool edited the scanner companion to show
a substantial long magnet body and deep bore, matching MRFoundry's revised mascot.
The village-at-dusk composition remains the visual reference. The two WebP files
are proportional 1,280- and 640-pixel-wide exports of that approved result.

Exact edit brief: preserve the twilight village, explorer, pond, bridge, lamps
and framing; replace the small shallow ring-shaped scanner beside the explorer
with a cream-and-blue miniature MRI scanner with a long cylindrical magnet,
deep tunnel, aligned table and small raised rear service cover. Use the revised
MRFoundry mascot as the geometry reference and match the scene's lighting.

`world-teaser.webp` and the images in `rooms/` are actual browser renders of the
current game, maintained separately from this entrance illustration.
