WELCOME, EXPLORER

Take the scenic route.

Walk up to a door to enter, and walk to the room’s exit to leave. You can also click a building to walk there. Each room has photographs, projects and research to discover. Your little MRI companion comes along.

Click the ground to walk, drag to look around, and scroll or pinch to zoom. You can also use WASD or the arrow keys while the world is focused. The camera follows you.

Select a framed picture to look closer, or press E or Enter beside it. Village is a shortcut back outside; Escape closes a note.

Press B for your companion backpack, or J for the field journal. MRI Scout follows by default; take other companions along whenever you like.

Turn on Music for a gentle soundtrack. It pauses when you switch away from this tab.
