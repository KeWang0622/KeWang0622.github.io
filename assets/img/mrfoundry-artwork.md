# MRFoundry MRI scanner artwork

Updated 3 November 2026 with the owner-approved cute MRI scanner mascot.
`mri-mascot.png` is the canonical transparent PNG, shared by the homepage,
Macintosh Research Lab, MRFoundry tutorial and the 3D world’s MRFoundry exhibit.
`mri-mascot-trimmed.png` removes only empty alpha padding and is the texture for
the MRI Scout and facade emblem. Favicons and social previews are size-only exports.
The outlined two-tone wordmark remains unchanged.

This is AI-generated brand artwork, not a scanner photograph, engineering drawing
or depiction of a validated clinical device. No patient data were used.
The artwork was generated and alpha-cleaned with OpenAI’s built-in image tool.
Exact prompts and canonical assets are maintained in
[MRFoundry assets](https://github.com/KeWang0622/MRFoundry/tree/main/assets).
