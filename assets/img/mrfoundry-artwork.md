# MRFoundry MRI scanner artwork

Updated 29 October 2026 with the project owner's requested MRI identity correction.
`mri-mascot.png` is the transparent decorative MRFoundry mascot shared by the
homepage, Macintosh Research Lab and the 3D world's MRFoundry exhibit.

The long cylindrical magnet housing, deep straight patient bore, aligned table,
small head-coil cradle and raised rear service cover replace the former shallow
ring silhouette. The cream/blue palette and friendly face remain consistent.
This is AI-generated brand artwork, not a scanner photograph, engineering drawing
or depiction of a validated clinical device. No patient data were used.

Generated with OpenAI's built-in image-generation tool from the previous
owner-approved artwork. Canonical assets and exact prompts are maintained in
[MRFoundry assets](https://github.com/KeWang0622/MRFoundry/tree/main/assets).
The 3D game companion is independently modeled geometry; its editable source and
provenance are in `pokemon/3d/assets/COMPANION-SOURCE.md`.
